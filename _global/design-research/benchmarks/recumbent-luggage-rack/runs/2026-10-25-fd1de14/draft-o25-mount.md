# Draft: a sound rack on the Baron's own rack points (leader O23a, fallback O25)

Throwaway draft of the leading option as the user would carry it out, to stress-test it on paper.

Where: the maker's rack points (Baron manual p. 12): the upper seat fixtures and the bolts half way down the seat
support tube. Not the mudguard holes above the dropouts ("not to be used for racks"), not a clamp on a frame tube.

1. Search a few weeks for an original Optima rack (Marktplaats, 2dehands, ligfiets.net forum, recumbent Facebook
   groups, ligfiets.shop's Optima parts). If found: it bolts to those points with the seat. Done; go to 4.
2. Otherwise a quality rack (Tubus/Racktime, 26") with short brackets to the same points:
   - upper struts to the upper seat fixtures, lower legs to the mid seat-support-tube bolts: two points far apart per
     side form a triangle, which is what stops sway;
   - flat aluminium or stainless brackets, metal-to-metal, M5/M6 stainless, threadlock; spherical washers where the
     angle doesn't match, so tightening doesn't bend the rack;
   - a cross-member between the two legs if the rack has none low down.
   Or have a ligfiets shop or framebuilder make the rack for these points (O23b).
3. The 6 Nm limit is for the bolts holding the seat shell on the seat frame, not the seat-frame fixtures the rack uses
   (manual p. 12); don't confuse the two. If a rubber spacer sits in the rack's joint, put the rack tab metal-to-metal
   on the frame side of it.
0. First, find where the current sway comes from (rack joints, lug bolts, tube clamp): it tells whether a new rack is
   needed or only a new mount.
4. Fit check with ~15 kg (the real use): grab the rack top and push sideways: no visible sway; ride a cobbled stretch:
   no rattle; tyre gap > 1 cm; panniers clear chain and brake; re-check all bolts after 50 km and after any seat-
   angle change.

Stress-test findings (paper):
- Sway comes from a rack fixed at points that don't form a triangle, or from joints without preload; both are what
  the current mount has (lugs + single tube clamp, F8). The F4 points give a triangle per side.
- Weak spot: the seat fixtures carry rider and rack, with a 6 Nm limit.
- Why the user might reject it: no Optima rack turns up, and making brackets is more work than they want → O23b.
- Not tested on the real bike.
