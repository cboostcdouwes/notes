Then the only change is longer struts. Your rack, the bolts by the rear axle and the clamps on the rear-fork tubes all stay as planned.

**Finding the right length:**
1. Clamp as **high on the rear-fork tubes** as you can. That keeps the struts short and steep, and steep struts brace the rack best.
2. Measure from the strut holes at the front of the rack top to that point on the tubes.
3. Choose the strut:
   - **Up to 350 mm:** [Tubus aluminium struts](https://www.rosebikes.com/p/tubus-mounting-strut-for-luggage-racks-218652), 350 mm, about €3–4 each.
   - **Longer:** two strips of flat aluminium or steel bar, about 20 × 3 mm, a few euros at a hardware store. Drill an M5 hole at each end. Drilling the strip doesn't change the bike.

**What you buy:** two struts or strips, a pair of Tubus seat-stay clamps sized to the tubes (about €10–24), and stainless M5 bolts. That comes to about €15–35, under your €50.

**Once fitted, check two things:**
- The struts sit at a steep angle, not long and flat.
- Pushing the rack top forwards and sideways doesn't make it move.

If it still flexes, clamp higher up the tubes or use the stiffer steel strip. The loaded test ride in `fit-check.md` comes last.
