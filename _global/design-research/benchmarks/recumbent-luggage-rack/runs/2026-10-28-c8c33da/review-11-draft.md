You may not need a strip at all. Whether you do depends on how long the struts must be, so please measure that first.

**Measure:** from the strut holes at the front of your rack top to the highest point on the rear-fork tubes where a clamp fits. A clamp high on the tubes keeps the distance short.

**What to do with that length:**
- **350 mm or less:** order two [Tubus aluminium struts](https://www.rosebikes.com/p/tubus-mounting-strut-for-luggage-racks-218652), 350 mm, about €3–4 each, online with the clamps. You don't need any other material.
- **Longer than 350 mm:** I found no strut longer than 350 mm for sale. Two ways around that:
  - **Lengthen your rack's own struts.** Overlap each one with a bought strut and join them with **two** M5 bolts. With one bolt the joint would hinge and the strut would buckle. This only works if your own struts are flat with holes. Round ones can't overlap firmly.
  - **Order perforated steel mounting strip online.** It's sold by the metre, already has holes, and you just cut it to length. It's flexible, so use it doubled over, or test that the rack doesn't sway.

**Two questions:**
1. How long does each strut need to be?
2. Are your rack's own struts flat with holes, or round rods?
