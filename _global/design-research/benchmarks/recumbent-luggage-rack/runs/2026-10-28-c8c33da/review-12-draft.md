At about 35 cm, two Tubus 350 mm struts should fit, but there's no margin.

Your own struts can't be reused. Round rods can't be overlapped firmly, so they can't be lengthened.

**Before ordering, check the length exactly:**
- **Measure precisely,** from the centre of the hole in the rack's tab to where the centre of the clamp's hole will be. "About 35 cm" could be a few mm over 350 mm.
- **Ask the seller** whether the 350 mm is measured between hole centres. I couldn't confirm that.
- **If the strut ends up a few mm short:** move the clamp a little along the tube, or turn it so its outer hole points towards the rack. I haven't verified how much that gains.

**Order (total about €17–35, under your €50):**
- 2× [Tubus aluminium strut, 350 mm](https://www.rosebikes.com/p/tubus-mounting-strut-for-luggage-racks-218652), about €3–4 each
- 1 pair of Tubus seat-stay clamps sized to your rear-fork tubes, about €10–24. Measure the tube diameter where the clamp goes; the sizes are 14, 16, 18–19, 21–22 and 24–25 mm.
- Stainless M5 bolts, with thread-locker for the bolts by the rear axle

**Fitting:**
1. Bolt your rack to the threaded holes by the rear axle.
2. Bolt the struts to the rack's tabs and to the clamps' outer holes.
3. Tighten the clamps only until they don't slip; too tight dents aluminium.
4. Push the rack top forwards and sideways: it shouldn't move.
5. Do the loaded test ride in `fit-check.md`, and re-tighten everything after 50 km.

**Known gaps:**
- Your rack's load rating; look for a stamp on it.
- The exact strut length.
- How the bike handles with the load, which the loaded test ride covers.
