Then buy two struts as well; your rack and the overall plan stay the same.

**What to buy:**
- **Two struts**, for example [Tubus aluminium struts](https://www.rosebikes.com/p/tubus-mounting-strut-for-luggage-racks-218652). They're 8 mm thick with an eye for an M5 bolt, come in 240 or 350 mm lengths, and cost about €3–4 each. Measure the distance first and pick the length that fits.
- **Tubus seat-stay clamps** sized to the rear-fork tubes, about €10–24 a pair.
- **Stainless M5 bolts.**
- **Total:** about €15–35, well under your €50.

**How it goes together:**
- The rack's legs bolt to the holes by the rear axle and carry the weight.
- The struts run from the front of the rack top to the rear-fork tubes and are clamped there. They only brace the rack.

**Two things to check on your rack:**
1. **Was it made to stand without struts, or were they lost?** Some racks are built to need none. If yours is one of those and holds steady on the lugs alone, the struts aren't needed.
2. **Does the front of the rack top have a hole or tab for a strut?** Tubus struts are made for Tubus and Racktime racks, so check one fits yours. If there's nowhere to bolt a strut, it can be fixed to the rack tube with a small rubber-lined clamp.

I couldn't find a universal adjustable strut pair with a confirmed price; the Tubus strut was the best lead.
