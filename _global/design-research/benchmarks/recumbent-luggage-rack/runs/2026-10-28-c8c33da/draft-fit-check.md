# Fit check: luggage on the Optima Baron

Do this on your own bike; about 15 minutes, a tape measure and a hex key set.

1. **Mounting points**: look for threaded holes (M5) at the rear dropouts and on the frame or seat brackets behind
   the seat. Photograph them. If you have the Optima rack, check all its bolts and spacers are there.
2. **Rack rating**: Optima rates its rack at 20 kg on the Baron. Your bags plus their contents must stay under that;
   aim for the heavy items at the bottom of the bags.
3. **Spoke and derailleur clearance**: hang the empty panniers on the rack and push them inwards: they must not reach
   the spokes or the rear derailleur. Panniers with a lower hook or strap stop them swinging in.
4. **Heel and hand clearance**: sit on the bike, turn the cranks backwards a full turn and check your heels and
   calves don't touch the bags; check you can still reach the seat bottom to get on and off.
5. **Under the seat and at the front wheel**: is there clearly no room for bags there? (Answers question 5.)
6. **Loaded test**: load as you would really ride, then: a tight turn at walking pace (no scraping), a short straight
   at speed with a light grip (no wobble), firm braking on a short descent. Re-tighten all bolts after the first
   50 km.
