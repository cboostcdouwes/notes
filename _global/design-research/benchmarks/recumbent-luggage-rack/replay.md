# Replay: questions and answers

Every question the skill asked, with the options offered and the user's answer verbatim, in order.

## Round 1
1. **Bike** — "Which recumbent is it (make/model, or at least its layout)? This decides whether a maker rack (O1)
   exists and which mount points, suspension and axle you have."
   Options: Known make/model · Short wheelbase, rear drive · Long wheelbase / trike · Front-wheel drive / lowracer.
   **Answer:** Known make/model.
2. **Load** — "What will you carry, and how often? This sets R1 and how much handling (R4) weighs."
   Options: Commute/shopping, <15 kg · Touring, 15-30 kg · Occasional big loads · Small items only.
   **Answer:** Commute/shopping, <15 kg.
3. **Rack?** — "Does it have to be a rack, or is any secure way of carrying the luggage fine?"
   Options: Any way is fine · It must be a rack · Rack preferred.
   **Answer (Other):** "I already have a rack, but other options are fine too I suppose."
4. **Remarks** — "Remarks? (e.g. rear suspension, warranty, kickstand, where the bike is stored or taken on trains,
   budget/DIY)"
   Options: No remarks · Remarks below.
   **Answer (Other):** "The bike is an Optima Baron"

Leader at the time of asking: O1 (maker's model-specific rack on designed mounts); runners-up O2, O20, O10.

## Round 2
1. **Your rack** — "Which rack do you already own?"
   Options: Original Optima rack · Generic upright rack · Another recumbent maker's · Don't know.
   **Answer:** Generic upright rack.
2. **Suspension** — "On your Baron: does the rear have suspension (a swingarm that moves), and is the tube with bolts
   half way down the seat support part of the main frame (it does not move with the rear wheel)?"
   Options: Yes, both · Suspension, but unsure · No rear suspension.
   **Answer:** No rear suspension.
3. **Maker rule** — "If a mount the manual says not to use (the swingarm holes above the dropouts) seemed to work in
   practice, would that be acceptable to you?"
   Options: No, follow the maker · Yes, if it holds · Depends on the reason.
   **Answer:** Yes, if it holds.
4. **Remarks** — "Remarks?" Options: No remarks · Remarks below.
   **Answer (Other):** "The bike already has a DIY rack mounted to the mudguard bolts, it seems to have held out fine."

Leader at the time of asking: O1 (rack on the maker's points: upper seat fixtures + mid seat-support-tube bolts);
O8 (tailbag) tied on requirements; O23, O4b close; O21 (swingarm/mudguard lugs + seat brace) trailing.
