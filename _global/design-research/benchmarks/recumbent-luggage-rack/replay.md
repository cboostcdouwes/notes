# Replay: questions and answers

Every question the skill asked, with the options offered and the user's answer verbatim, in order.

## Round 1
1. **Bike** — "Which recumbent is it (make/model, or at least its layout)? This decides whether a maker rack (O1)
   exists and which mount points, suspension and axle you have."
   Options: Known make/model · Short wheelbase, rear drive · Long wheelbase / trike · Front-wheel drive / lowracer.
   **Answer:** Known make/model.
2. **Load** — "What will you carry, and how often? This sets R1 and how much handling (R4) weighs."
   Options: Commute/shopping, <15 kg · Touring, 15-30 kg · Occasional big loads · Small items only.
   **Answer:** Commute/shopping, <15 kg.
3. **Rack?** — "Does it have to be a rack, or is any secure way of carrying the luggage fine?"
   Options: Any way is fine · It must be a rack · Rack preferred.
   **Answer (Other):** "I already have a rack, but other options are fine too I suppose."

   budget/DIY)"
   Options: No remarks · Remarks below.
   **Answer (Other):** "The bike is an Optima Baron"

Leader at the time of asking: O1 (maker's model-specific rack on designed mounts); runners-up O2, O20, O10.
