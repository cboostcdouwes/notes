# Recumbent luggage carrying

## Problem
The user rides a recumbent bicycle and wants to carry luggage on it. The user's request named the solution ("attach a
luggage rack") and asked how best to attach it; the need behind it is that luggage travels with the bike and rider,
held securely, without making the bike unsafe or unpleasant to ride. What luggage (volume, weight, how often), which
recumbent (layout, frame material, suspension, existing mounting points) and what riding (commute, touring, shopping)
are not known yet.

## Related decisions
None yet. Possible child decision once the carrying approach is chosen: the exact mounting hardware for the user's
frame.

## Success criteria
- Judged by the user, who knows the expected answer (benchmark run): does the leading option match it, and in which
  round did it lead?
- Judged in use: the loaded bike rides without the load or carrier shifting, rubbing or touching wheel, chain,
  rider's heels or arms; mounting points show no cracks or loosening after a season; mounting and dismounting the
  bike are no harder than before.
